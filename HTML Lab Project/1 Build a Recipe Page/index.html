Mutton Curry

A hearty North Indian dish, mutton curry is a flavorful combination of tender mutton pieces simmered in aromatic spices, onions, tomatoes, and yogurt. Serve it with rice or roti for a comforting meal.

Ingredients

  • 500g mutton (bone-in cubes, washed)
  • 2 large onions, finely sliced
  • 2 tomatoes, chopped
  • 2 tbsp ginger-garlic paste
  • 1 cup thick yogurt
  • 3 tbsp cooking oil or ghee

Instructions

  1. Heat oil/ghee in a heavy-bottomed pan. Add cumin seeds and bay leaves, sauté for 30 seconds.
  2. Add sliced onions, cook until golden brown. Mix in ginger-garlic paste, sauté for 2-3 minutes.
  3. dd tomatoes and cook until soft. Add turmeric, coriander powder, chili powder, and salt; cook spices till oil separates.
  4. Add mutton pieces. Stir well to coat with spices, cook for 5 minutes.
  5. Pour in yogurt and mix thoroughly. Cover and cook on low heat for 30-40 minutes, stirring occasionally.
  6. Once mutton is tender, add garam masala and cook for 5 more minutes.
  7. arnish with fresh coriander leaves and serve hot with rice or roti.
